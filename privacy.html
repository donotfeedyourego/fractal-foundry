Fractal Foundry: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

The short version

Fractal Foundry does not collect, send or share any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, trackers or in-app purchases.

What is stored, and where

Your game progress is saved only on your own device. That includes unlocked concepts, your Future Garden, settings, favourite cards and the names typed in for Duo mode. It never leaves the device, and we cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Internet and permissions

The game works fully offline. It does not ask for your location, camera, microphone, contacts or any other device permission.

Children

Fractal Foundry is designed for players aged about 10 to 16. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we collect none from children. This meets the requirements of COPPA, GDPR-K and similar laws.

Third parties

No third-party services or software development kits (SDKs) are included in the app, and no data is shared with anyone.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
