How It Works

Move→Choose→Discover→Bloom→Combo→New zone

Controls

Steer

←→ or AD to switch lanes. On touch: ◀ ▶ buttons or swipe.

Jump

↑ W or Space to hop low beams. Touch: JUMP, tap or swipe up.

Dash

↓ S or Shift — a burst of speed that phases through anything. Short cooldown.

Phase shift

E or Q (touch: PHASE) flips you between dimension α and β. Tinted walls exist in only one dimension: shift away and slip straight through.

Pause & sound

P/Esc to pause, M to mute.

The Foundry

🜂 Learning gates

Every so often, three portals appear with a quick question. Steer into the right answer. Correct answers make the world bloom and power you up for a few seconds.

✦ Concept crafting

Grab glowing fragments like gravity or orbit. Hold two that fit together and you'll make a discovery.

◎ Skill rifts

Golden rings are optional side routes: dive in for two quick bonus gates worth double.

♡ Gentle misses

Wrong answers don't hurt. The right portal glows, a recovery lane opens, and the explanation waits for you in your debrief. Nothing pops up mid-ride.

⬡ Five zones

Number Jungle, Circuit Ocean, Word Volcano, Orbit Garden and Logic Glacier. Reach a zone and you can start there next time.

◆ Energy

Crashing into tall blocks costs one energy. Lose all three and the run ends. Green orbs refill it.

⚡ Insight

Some right answers charge ⚡ Insight: your next jump becomes a soaring leap or your next dash a long smash. The "why it works" is saved for your debrief.

⬡ Dimensions 5–9

Each zone is a higher dimension, from D5 Number Jungle to D9 Logic Glacier. Their hypercubes turn in the sky, and an echo track hangs overhead.

⟲ Dimension folds

Fly through a fold ring and the whole world barrel-rolls into a mirrored palette.

✎ Forge seeds

Rainbow hexagons hold creative challenges: wire a circuit, balance a meadow, order a story, build a bridge, echo a signal. Catch them and solve them calmly in your Future Garden.

⚠ Zone cores

Each zone ends at a core. Fly through to capture its echo (a system puzzle for later), then steer into one of three dimension doors to pick where you go next.

🌍 Future Garden

Your own planet. What you learn becomes its laws: clouds, rivers, plants, moons, grids and story stones appear. Forecast +10 to +1,000 years and solve one mystery at a time.

🧩 My Worlds

Discoveries and solved puzzles unlock pieces for your Solar-System Garden, Circuit Reef and Word City. Every piece holds a fact.

✧ Curiosity cards

Grab spinning ? cards to collect surprising real-world facts, and star your favourites.

🤝 Duo mode

Pass the phone between runs! One player pilots, the other navigates, and you take turns on Garden puzzles toward a shared team goal.

☕ Healthy play

After about 10 minutes, an optional Chapter Harbor appears on the track. Garden changes end each chapter with a calm stopping point. No streaks, no daily pressure.
